Where this comes from

Every record links to its original source. Grants come from funding-agency databases (NSF, NIH RePORTER, UKRI). Positions come from university pages and academic job boards. Research profiles come from OpenAlex. We never copy personal email addresses; contact supervisors through their university page.

Scholarship amounts and deadlines change every year. Always confirm on the official page before you apply.

Data sources and disclaimer

PhD Mentor Finder is an independent app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or acting on behalf of any government agency, including the National Institutes of Health (NIH), the National Science Foundation (NSF) or UK Research and Innovation (UKRI), or with any university, job board or scholarship programme listed in the app.

Grant information comes from these public government databases:

  • NIH RePORTER: reporter.nih.gov
  • NSF Award Search: nsf.gov/awardsearch
  • UKRI Gateway to Research: gtr.ukri.org

Publication data comes from OpenAlex (CC0). Always check details with the official source before relying on them.

What the supervisor labels mean

Hiring now The supervisor is named on a currently open, advertised position.

Funds students They lead a grant whose purpose is paying graduate students or trainees.

Active grant They hold a current research grant. Many such labs take students, but it is not guaranteed.
