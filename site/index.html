Where this comes from

Every item shows its source and when it was last updated, and links to the original page. Grants come from public funding-agency databases. Positions come from university pages and academic job boards that allow it. Research profiles and recent papers come from OpenAlex. We never store personal email addresses; contact supervisors through their official university page.

Amounts and deadlines change. Always confirm on the official page before you apply.

Data sources and disclaimer

PhD Mentor Finder is an independent app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or acting on behalf of any government agency, including the National Institutes of Health (NIH), the National Science Foundation (NSF) or UK Research and Innovation (UKRI), or with any university, job board or scholarship programme listed in the app.

Grant and position information comes from these public government databases:

  • NIH RePORTER: reporter.nih.gov
  • NSF Award Search: nsf.gov/awardsearch
  • UKRI Gateway to Research: gtr.ukri.org

Publication data comes from OpenAlex (CC0). Subjects follow the OpenAlex field and subfield classification. Always check details with the official source before relying on them.

What the supervisor labels mean

Hiring now Named on a currently open, advertised position.

Active grant Holds a current research grant. Many such labs take students, but it is not guaranteed.
