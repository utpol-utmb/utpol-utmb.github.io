PhD Mentor Finder helps students find a PhD or Master's supervisor who is actually taking students, and the funding to pay for it. Search by topic, supervisor or university, then filter by country, region, subject area, degree, start year and deadline.

What you can find:
• Supervisors with open, advertised positions
• Supervisors who hold grants that pay graduate students
• Funded PhD and Master's positions with stipend, duration, start date and deadline
• Scholarships such as Chevening, Commonwealth, DAAD, Erasmus Mundus, Fulbright, MEXT and more, with amounts, eligibility and deadlines

Every supervisor carries a clear label:
• Hiring now: named on a currently open advert
• Funds students: leads a training grant or graduate fellowship
• Active grant: holds a current research grant, so ask first

How it works:
• Grant records come from public databases published by funding agencies (NIH, NSF and UKRI); positions come from university pages and academic job boards
• Every record links to its original source
• Updated automatically every day; closed deadlines disappear
• Scholarships show whether details were confirmed on the official site

Save supervisors, positions and scholarships to your shortlist with one tap. No sign-up, no ads, no tracking.
