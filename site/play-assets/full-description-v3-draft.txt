PhD Mentor Finder helps students find a PhD or Master's supervisor who is actually taking students, and the funding to pay for it, in every field from public health and biology to engineering, computer science, social sciences and the humanities.

Search by topic, supervisor or university, browse by field and subfield, and filter by country, degree, start year, deadline, funding and whether international applicants can apply.

What you can find:
• Supervisors with open, advertised positions
• Supervisors who hold grants that pay graduate students
• Funded PhD and Master's positions with stipend, start date and deadline
• Scholarships such as Chevening, Commonwealth, DAAD, Erasmus Mundus, Fulbright, MEXT and more, with amounts, eligibility and deadlines

For each supervisor: research topics, recent papers, active funding, open positions, and a quick way to find their official university profile, plus a short guide and an email-draft helper for contacting them.

Every supervisor carries a clear label:
• Hiring now: named on a currently open advert
• Funds students: leads a training grant or graduate fellowship
• Active grant: holds a current research grant, so ask first

Plan your applications:
• Save supervisors, positions and scholarships to My list
• Track each one (interested, contacted, applied, interview, offer) with private notes
• Add deadlines to your calendar with reminders
• Save searches and see new matches each time you open the app

How it works:
• Grant records come from public databases published by funding agencies; positions come from university pages and academic job boards that allow it
• Every record links to its original source and shows when it was last checked
• Updated automatically every day; closed deadlines disappear

No sign-up, no ads, no tracking. Your lists, notes and searches stay on your phone.
