Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026

The short version

PhD Mentor Finder does not ask you to sign in, does not collect your personal information, and does not use advertising or tracking.

What stays on your device

When you save a supervisor, position or scholarship with the star button, that list is stored only in your own browser or app on your device. It is never sent to us. Clearing your browser or app data removes it.

What we do not collect

  • No accounts, names, email addresses or phone numbers.
  • No location, contacts, camera or microphone access.
  • No analytics, advertising identifiers or third-party trackers.

Services the app uses

The app is hosted on GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, may keep standard server logs such as IP addresses for security. See GitHub's privacy statement. Page fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. Links to universities, job boards and funding agencies open their own websites, which have their own privacy policies.

Information about researchers

The app lists supervisors using public, professional information only: names, institutions, public grant records from funding agencies, advertised positions, and public publication records from OpenAlex. We never store personal email addresses or private details. A researcher who wants their listing corrected or removed can contact us at the address below, and we will act within 14 days.

Children

The app is intended for university graduates and is not directed at children under 13.
