Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026 (v3.1)

The short version

PhD Mentor Finder does not ask you to sign in, does not collect your personal information, and does not use advertising or tracking.

What stays on your device

Your saved list, saved searches, application statuses, private notes, search preferences (including the citizenship you choose to enter) and anything you type into the email-draft helper (such as your name and background) are stored only in your own browser or app on your device. They are never sent to us or anyone else. Clearing your browser or app data removes them; use "Download my list" if you want a copy. Calendar files and share links are created on your device, and you decide where to send them. "Report an error" opens your own email app.

What we do not collect

  • No accounts, names, email addresses or phone numbers.
  • No location, contacts, camera or microphone access.
  • No analytics, advertising identifiers or third-party trackers.

Services the app uses

The app is hosted on GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, may keep standard server logs such as IP addresses for security. See GitHub's privacy statement. Page fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. Links to universities, job boards and funding agencies open their own websites, which have their own privacy policies.

Information about researchers

The app lists supervisors using public, professional information only: names, institutions, public grant records from funding agencies, advertised positions, and public publication records from OpenAlex (titles of recent papers, research topics and citation counts). Subject tags are generated automatically from those records. We never store personal email addresses or private details. A researcher who wants their listing corrected or removed can contact us at the address below, and we will act within 14 days.

For researchers in Europe (and everyone listed)

If you are a researcher, your name, institution, department, research topics, recent publications, grants and any advertised positions may appear in this app. We take these details only from public professional sources: funding-agency grant records, job adverts your institution published, and OpenAlex publication records. Where an official record itself publishes a work email (for example an NSF award record or a job advert's contact line), we show that email with its source next to it; we never guess or construct an email address. We do not collect anything about you that is not already public in those sources, and we do not sell or share it.

Why we use it: to help students find supervisors who may be recruiting, which we consider a legitimate interest of students and researchers alike (EU/UK GDPR Article 6(1)(f)). Under the GDPR you have the right to know what we show about you, to have it corrected, and to object to it being shown. To exercise any of these rights, use “Report an error or request removal” on your entry in the app, or email the address below. We act within 14 days, and a removed entry or email stays hidden in future updates. You can also complain to your national data protection authority.

Data sources and disclaimer

PhD Mentor Finder is an independent app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or acting on behalf of any government agency, including the National Institutes of Health (NIH), the National Science Foundation (NSF) or UK Research and Innovation (UKRI), or with any university, job board or scholarship programme listed in the app. Grant information comes from these public government databases:

  • NIH RePORTER: https://reporter.nih.gov
  • NSF Award Search: https://www.nsf.gov/awardsearch/
  • UKRI Gateway to Research: https://gtr.ukri.org

Publication data comes from OpenAlex. Always check details with the official source before relying on them. See also the Terms of use.

Children

The app is intended for university graduates and is not directed at children under 13.
