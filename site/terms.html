What this app is

PhD Mentor Finder is a free, independent directory that helps students find possible PhD and Master's supervisors, funded positions and scholarships. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or acting on behalf of any government agency (including NIH, NSF and UKRI), university, job board or scholarship programme. Names of agencies, universities and programmes are used only to say where information comes from.

Information, not advice

Listings are collected automatically or by hand from public sources and may be incomplete, out of date or wrong. Subject tags are generated automatically and may be imperfect. A grant or listing does not mean a supervisor will accept you, and nothing in the app is an offer of a place or funding. Always confirm deadlines, eligibility and amounts on the official page before you apply. Amounts shown in US dollars are rough conversions for comparison only.

Sources

Grant records come from NIH RePORTER (https://reporter.nih.gov), NSF Award Search (https://www.nsf.gov/awardsearch/) and UKRI Gateway to Research (https://gtr.ukri.org), which are public records of their respective governments. Publication data comes from OpenAlex, released under CC0. Positions and scholarships link to the original university, job-board or programme page, which remains the authoritative version. We show short factual summaries and link out; we do not copy full adverts.

Using the app

You may use the app for your own study and job search. The email-draft helper only suggests text; you write and send any email yourself, from your own account, to the address on the supervisor's official page. Please do not use it to send bulk or unsolicited messages to researchers, or to copy the whole dataset for resale.

Corrections and removal

If something is wrong, or you are a researcher who wants a listing corrected or removed, email the address below. We act on requests within 14 days.
